# Why notes and articles together

Most tools force a choice: microblog *or* long-form. nitpub keeps both first-class on one federated identity.

## What you get

- Short notes that federate like statuses
- Articles with a proper permalink and truncation-friendly summary for remote readers
- Moderated inbound replies on the post thread

## Try the install

The quick install is a one-liner on a Debian/Ubuntu VPS — see the [install docs](https://docs.nitpub.com/guide/install).
