Own your notes and articles on the fediverse

One Go binary on a small VPS. Short notes and long-form posts, federated replies, without running Mastodon.

Built for indie operators

  • Notes + articles Both first-class — not bolted onto a microblog or split across tools.
  • ActivityPub replies Federated comments land in moderation, then on the permalink thread.
  • Budget VPS footprint Single binary, Caddy-friendly install, Releases instead of build-on-box.
  • Your domain Identity at @you@your.domain — not a rented silo.
